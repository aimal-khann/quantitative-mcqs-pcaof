5-Step Solving Method

A Simple Routine for Every Question

01

Read Carefully

Read the last question line carefully: does it ask for what is left, unchecked items, percentage points, or a decision?

02

Pick Right Numbers

Underline only the numbers you need. Check if setup hours, unpaid orders, or a non-math rule also apply.

03

Convert Units

Make units match before calculating: minutes into hours, words into tokens, or vendors at the same item count.

04

Solve Step by Step

Simplify fractions first, use 10%/25%/50% shortcuts, and finish the final subtraction or comparison.

05

Double-Check

Does your answer make sense? Did you divide by the right starting total? Does the "because" reason match?

Percentages & Rate Analysis

% = (Part ÷ Total) × 100 · % Change = (New − Old) ÷ Old

Quick Tips
  • 10% = ÷10, 5% = half of 10%
  • 25% = ÷4, 50% = ÷2
  • Two % changes in a row? Try starting with 100!
Common Traps
  • Dividing by the new number instead of the old one
  • Mixing up % points with real % change
  • Adding +50% and −40% straight together
◎ Topic 2 • Q13–Q24 Practice →

Sampling & Error Analysis

Error Rate = Errors ÷ Checked · Expected = Rate × Target Group

Quick Tips
  • See how many times bigger the unread set is (e.g. 900 ÷ 300 = 3×)
  • Multiply by the rate, not just the raw error count
  • Check weak groups (like handwritten forms) separately
Common Traps
  • Counting already-fixed sample items in the "remaining errors"
  • Assuming 0 errors in 50 items proves 0% errors overall
  • Averaging two group rates when one group is huge
÷ Topic 3 • Q25–Q36 Practice →

Fractions & Multi-Step

1/2 = 50% · 1/4 = 25% · 1/5 = 20% · 1/8 = 12.5%

Quick Tips
  • Divide first, then multiply
  • Find what is left right away (1/5 removed → 4/5 stays)
  • Add fractions using the same bottom number
Common Traps
  • Taking the 2nd fraction from the original total instead of what was left
  • Stopping after step 1 (reporting what was removed instead of what stays)
⏱ Topic 4 • Q37–Q48 Practice →

Time, Cost & Efficiency

Saving = Old Time − Your AI Time · Hours = Minutes ÷ 60

Quick Tips
  • Find saving per item first, then multiply
  • Subtract one-time setup hours when asked for net saving
  • Change words to tokens using (Words ÷ 3) × 4
Common Traps
  • Counting AI working time as your own work time
  • Forgetting human checking or fixing costs
  • Leaving your answer in minutes when it asks for hours
⚖ Topic 5 • Q49–Q60 Practice →

Budget & Comparative Decisions

Real Total Cost = Fees + Error Fixes · Budget Used = Paid + Unpaid Invoices + Open Orders

Quick Tips
  • Compare two vendors at the exact same volume (like per 1,000)
  • Include error-fixing cost, not just the sticker price
  • Run the ×10 check: if bigger numbers don't change the answer, a rule decides!
Common Traps
  • Comparing one vendor on easy tasks with another on hard mixed tasks
  • Picking the cheaper fee while ignoring costly mistakes
  • Approving a plan that fits the budget when a freeze or privacy rule blocks it
Interactive Trap Guide

9 Common Mistakes to Watch Out For (Click Any Card to See the Fix)

Trap 01 • Wrong Total

Dividing by the Wrong Number

"Of the claims NOT accepted, what % were rejected?" compares against 160, not all 400.

Click to see the Safe Fix →
Safe Fix: Check what comes right after "of" or "from" — divide by that group's total, or by the starting number for % change.
Trap 02 • Points vs. %

Mixing Up Percentage Points and %

Accuracy rising from 80% to 92% is +12 percentage points, NOT a 12% increase.

Click to see the Safe Fix →
Safe Fix: Points = simple subtraction (92 − 80 = 12 pp). Real % increase = 12 ÷ 80 = 15%.
Trap 03 • Back-to-Back %

Adding +50% and −40% Directly

Going up 50% and then dropping 40% does NOT leave you 10% higher!

Click to see the Safe Fix →
Safe Fix: Start with 100: +50% makes 150. Then −40% of 150 removes 60, leaving 90 (10% lower!).
Trap 04 • Checked vs. Unchecked

Counting Already-Fixed Errors Again

If 24 errors in 600 checked lines are already fixed, only the 1,400 unchecked lines still have errors.

Click to see the Safe Fix →
Safe Fix: Read carefully: does it ask about the whole batch or only the unchecked/unread part?
Trap 05 • Stopping Early

Picking Step 1 Instead of What Stays

When asked "how many files stay?", don't stop after finding how many were removed!

Click to see the Safe Fix →
Safe Fix: Always finish the last subtraction — or use what remains (3/4 of 40 = 30, then 4/5 of 30 = 24).
Trap 06 • Hidden Time & Cost

Forgetting Setup Time or Human Checking

Saving 120 hours over a year minus a 6-hour setup is 114 net hours saved, not 120.

Click to see the Safe Fix →
Safe Fix: Subtract one-time setup hours from savings, and add human fix labor ($/hr × fix hours) to AI fees.
Trap 07 • Wrong Units

Mixing Words with Tokens or Minutes with Hours

You cannot subtract 600,000 words straight from a 1,000,000-token limit.

Click to see the Safe Fix →
Safe Fix: Convert words to tokens first: (600,000 ÷ 3) × 4 = 800,000 tokens. Always match units first!
Trap 08 • Unfair Compare

Comparing Easy Work with Hard Work

5% errors on easy invoices vs. 6% errors on mixed hard invoices cannot tell you which vendor is better.

Click to see the Safe Fix →
Safe Fix: Rates adjust for batch size, NOT task difficulty. Only rank vendors tested on the same difficulty!
Trap 09 • Non-Math Rule

Doing Math When a Rule Decides

Being under a $300k budget still doesn't allow hiring if management froze hiring last week.

Click to see the Safe Fix →
Safe Fix: Use the ×10 test: if changing numbers 10× doesn't change the decision, follow the rule, risk, or privacy tier!
